User Data Deletion Instructions

How to request deletion of information handled by MA Page Publisher.

1. Remove app access

If you authorised MA Page Publisher through Facebook, you can remove its access from the relevant Facebook account or business integration settings where it appears. Removing access can prevent future data access by the app.

2. Request deletion of app-held data

Send an email to epicaigoals2026@gmail.com with the subject MA Page Publisher - Data Deletion Request. Include the Facebook Page name or ID involved and a description of the data you would like removed. Do not email passwords or access tokens.

3. Verification and processing

We may ask for enough information to confirm that you are authorised to make the request. We will review the request, delete information under our control where appropriate and respond with an outcome. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

4. Facebook-hosted content

Content already published on Facebook is managed through Facebook's tools. Deleting data held by this integration does not automatically remove a Reel or post from Facebook. A Page administrator may need to delete that content separately.

5. Contact

For questions or follow-up, email epicaigoals2026@gmail.com.

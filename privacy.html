Privacy Policy

How MA Page Publisher handles information used for Facebook Page publishing.

Effective date: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

MA Page Publisher is a tool for authorised Facebook Page administrators to publish and manage Page content. For privacy questions, contact epicaigoals2026@gmail.com.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features enabled and the permissions granted to the app, MA Page Publisher may process Page identifiers and names, granted permissions, access tokens needed to authenticate with Meta APIs, content or media selected for publication, and publication identifiers or status responses.

The app is intended to work with information needed for the requested Page publishing operation, rather than requesting unrelated personal information.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorised Page activity, submit content for publishing, check the result of publishing requests, address errors and maintain the security of the integration. It is not used for unrelated advertising or sold to data brokers.

4. Where information is processed

Information is processed through Meta's platform and the computing environment used to operate the integration. Access tokens and other credentials must be kept private and accessible only to authorised operators. Data that Meta retains under its own services is subject to Meta's applicable privacy terms.

5. Sharing

Information required to perform the requested operation is sent to Meta through its APIs. Information may also be accessible to the operator's authorised hosting or technical service providers, where used to run the integration. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention

Operational information is kept only for as long as necessary to perform publishing, investigate problems, meet legal obligations or maintain required security records. Revoking app permissions stops future authorised API access, but content already published to Facebook may remain until removed through Facebook or by an authorised Page administrator.

7. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect tokens and other information, including restricting access and avoiding disclosure of credentials in public repositories or published pages. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and deletion requests

Authorised users may withdraw the app's access using their applicable Facebook settings. To request deletion of information held by the integration, use our data deletion instructions or contact epicaigoals2026@gmail.com. We may need sufficient details to verify the request. Removing content from Facebook itself may require using Facebook's tools.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the integration changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
